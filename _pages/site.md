This is the website of Pei Li's research group at the University of Wyoming.

The design and layouts are adapted from [Blotter](https://github.com/blab/blotter), the open-source template behind the [Bedford Lab website](https://bedford.io/), by Trevor Bedford. Blotter's source code is used under the MIT license. The site uses Bootstrap and Font Awesome, with locally hosted styles and icons.

Publication records, research descriptions, team information, and announcements belong to this research group. Sponsor logos link to their respective organizations.
