I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Wyoming. My research models the safety of cyber-physical transportation systems with AI-based methods. I develop models and applications that can sense traffic, predict future conditions, and make decisions. My research bridges the gap between transportation engineering and cutting-edge technologies, including digital twins, AI, and V2X.
