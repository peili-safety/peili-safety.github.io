Safety modeling leverages advanced AI and data-driven techniques to understand and predict safety in real-time for infrastructure, vehicles, and road users. By integrating machine learning models with real-time data, we aim to enhance safety measures and reduce risks. Our research focuses on developing innovative approaches to predict crash likelihood, detect driving maneuvers, and improve spatiotemporal transferability of safety models. These efforts contribute to creating safer transportation systems and environments for all users. Some challegenges include:

  • Real-time data integration and analysis
  • Model transferability across different contexts
  • Handling complex interactions between infrastructure, vehicles, and road users
