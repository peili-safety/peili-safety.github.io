## Project summary

Led by Dr. Pei Li at the University of Wyoming, this project will combine telematics, crash, roadway, and weather data with stakeholder input to support safety planning. The work will examine driver behavior and commercial vehicle crash patterns and develop recommendations for safer roads and freight corridors. The project is selected for USDOT's **FY 2026 Safe Streets and Roads for All (SS4A)** program.
